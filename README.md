### Project Description
A completely unnecessary AI-powered website that pretends to detect your brain activity using your webcam and facial detection. It generates scientifically questionable percentages for brain activity, focus, overthinking, sleepiness, distraction, and other extremely important things nobody asked to measure. 😂

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
People have no idea how much of their brain they are using while doing absolutely nothing.
This creates a serious and completely imaginary problem: “Am I actually thinking right now?” 

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We built the Brain Activity Detector™ — a sophisticated-looking system that uses your webcam and face detection to perform an absolutely meaningless “neural analysis.”
